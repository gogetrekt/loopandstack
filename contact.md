## Email

The best way to reach the loop and stack team is by email:

**nethroes@proton.me**

We read everything that arrives there. Please use a clear subject line; it helps us route your message to the writer who covers that beat.

## What to write about

- **Corrections.** If an article has a wrong price, an outdated feature, or any other error, tell us the article URL and what is wrong. Corrections are fixed in the open and noted on the article, as described in our [Editorial Policy](/editorial-policy/).
- **Review requests and tips.** Suggest a tool we should review or compare. We cannot cover everything, and coverage is never for sale; see the [Affiliate Disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).
- **Business and advertising.** Questions about the site, partnerships, and advertising.
