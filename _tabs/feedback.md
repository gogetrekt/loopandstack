# Reader feedback

This page is only for suggestions and criticism from readers. Found a mistake in an article? Think a comparison was unfair, a price was wrong, or the site is hard to read on your phone? Tell us here. Praise is welcome too, but honest criticism is what actually improves the site.

Every message lands in our editorial inbox. We read all of it. We cannot reply to everyone, and product pitches or link requests sent through this form are deleted unread. For anything else, use the [Contact](/contact/) page instead.
