Loop and Stack is an independent tech publication about tools: software, AI tools, developer tools, and security tools. Plain English, current prices, no hype.

## The team

Our writers publish under editorial pen names and each covers a fixed beat:

- **Daniel Reyes** — AI tools and general software. Comparisons, pricing breakdowns, and best-of lists.
- **Priya Nair** — Developer tools. Editors, terminals, automation, and anything that ships code.
- **Tomas Silva** — Security tools. VPNs, password managers, and privacy software, tested against the claims on the box.

## How we work

Every article is researched fresh from primary sources: official docs, changelogs, GitHub releases, and vendor pricing pages, checked the same day it is published. Reviews use a fixed format with a quick verdict, pros and cons, and a straight answer on who should buy and who should skip. The full method is on our [Editorial Policy](/editorial-policy/) page.

Some links are affiliate links and we say so openly. Rankings are never for sale. See the [Affiliate Disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).

## Contact and corrections

Spotted an error, or want a tool covered? Open an issue on our GitHub repository at github.com/gogetrekt/loopandstack, or see the [Contact](/contact/) page. Corrections are fixed in the open and noted on the article.
