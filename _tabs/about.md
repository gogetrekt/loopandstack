Loop and Stack is an independent tech publication about tools: software, AI tools, developer tools, and security tools.

Our articles are written by an editorial team under pen names. Each writer covers a fixed beat and keeps a consistent voice. We research from primary sources first, meaning official documentation, changelogs, GitHub releases, and vendor pricing pages, and we link those sources in every article.

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you buy through them, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Affiliate relationships never decide our rankings; prices and claims are checked against the vendor's own pages on the day an article is published.

Corrections: if you spot an error, open an issue on our GitHub repository at github.com/gogetrekt/loopandstack and we will fix it in the open.
