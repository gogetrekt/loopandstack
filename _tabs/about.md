Loop and Stack is an independent tech publication about tools: software, AI tools, developer tools, and security tools. Plain English, current prices, no hype.

## What we cover

We help readers choose the right tool and get the most out of it:

- **Reviews** of individual tools, with a quick verdict, pros and cons, and current pricing.
- **Comparisons** that put two or three tools side by side on the things that actually differ.
- **Tutorials** written against the current version of a tool, not a screenshot from three releases ago.
- **Pricing breakdowns** that translate plan pages into what you really pay at each tier.

## The team

Our writers publish under editorial pen names, and each covers a fixed beat:

- **Daniel Reyes, AI Tools and Software Editor.** Covers AI assistants, writing and productivity software, and SaaS pricing. Writes the comparisons and best-of lists.
- **Priya Nair, Developer Tools Editor.** Covers editors, terminals, automation platforms, and anything that ships code. Writes the hands-on tutorials.
- **Tomas Silva, Security Tools Editor.** Covers VPNs, password managers, and privacy software. Tests marketing claims against what the product actually does.

## How we work

Every article is researched fresh on the day it is published, from primary sources: official documentation, changelogs, GitHub releases, and vendor pricing pages. Prices and plan limits are checked at publication and again when an article is updated. Reviews follow a fixed format so readers can compare like with like, and every article links its sources. The full method, including how we use AI tools and how corrections work, is on our [Editorial Policy](/editorial-policy/) page.

## Independence

Loop and Stack is reader-funded through advertising and affiliate commissions. Vendors cannot pay for coverage, placement, or a better verdict, and we do not accept sponsored reviews. When a link can earn us a commission, the article says so. See the [Affiliate Disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).
