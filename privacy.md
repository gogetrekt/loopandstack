Last updated: November 20, 2026.

This policy explains what information is collected when you visit loop and stack (loopandstack.tech), how it is used, and the choices you have. loop and stack is a static website: we run no user accounts, no comment system, and no contact forms, and we do not ask you for personal information to read the site.

## Information collected automatically

Like almost every website, the infrastructure that serves this site (GitHub Pages) may process standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages requested, in server logs used to operate and secure the service. GitHub's handling of that data is covered by GitHub's own privacy statement.

If we use analytics, it is limited to aggregated page view statistics. We do not build profiles of individual visitors, and we do not sell personal data to anyone.

## Advertising and cookies

When ads are enabled, this site uses Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other sites on the internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits here and elsewhere.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings at adssettings.google.com. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.

## Affiliate links

Some outbound links are affiliate links (see our [Affiliate Disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/)). When you click one, the vendor or its affiliate network may set a cookie so a resulting purchase can be attributed to us. Those cookies are controlled by the vendor's or network's own privacy policy, which applies once you leave this site.

## Email

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it only to reply and to handle the subject of your message, such as a correction. We do not add email addresses to marketing lists.

## Children

This site is written for a general adult audience of tool users and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top. Material changes will be noted on the page.
