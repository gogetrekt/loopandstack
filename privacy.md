Loop and Stack (loopandstack.tech) is a static website. We do not run user accounts and we do not collect personal information directly through this site.

## Advertising and cookies

When ads are enabled, this site uses Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other sites on the internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits here and elsewhere.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings (adssettings.google.com). You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.

## Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics that aggregate page views. We do not sell personal data, and we do not build profiles of individual visitors.

## Affiliate links

Some outbound links are affiliate links. The destination sites have their own privacy policies and cookie practices. See our [Affiliate Disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).

## Your choices

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies in settings. Blocking cookies will not break this site.
